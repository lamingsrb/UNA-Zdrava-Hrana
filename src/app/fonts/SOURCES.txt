Fonts are bundled to keep production builds independent of Google Fonts responses.
Original variable font outlines and glyph coverage are preserved; TTF files are compressed losslessly to WOFF2 with fontTools/Brotli.
Both families are licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see included licenses).
